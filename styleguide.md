# 🌄 Guru Nanak Dev Ji – The Four Udasis

### Visual Style & Art Direction Document

**Creative Direction:** Retro-Futuristic Spiritual Journey  
**Created by:** Amandeep Singh  
**Purpose:** To establish a consistent and respectful visual style for a scrollable, animated web experience depicting Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s four Udasis through cinematic, symbolic art.

---

## 🎨 OVERALL STYLE SUMMARY

**Style Name:** Retro-Futuristic Spiritual Realism  
**Inspiration:**

- Retro sci-fi poster aesthetics (like _Prometheus Fuel from the Air_)
- 70s pulp comic realism
- Indian sacred art symbolism
- Grainy cinematic matte paintings

**Mood:**

> A timeless traveler whose presence becomes one with the lands he visits — blending realism, symbolism, and divinity through motion and atmosphere.

---

## 🪶 VISUAL PRINCIPLES

- **No direct depiction** of Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s face.  
  Always shown **from the back**, in silhouette, or through **radiant aura and environment merging**.
- The journey is represented through **land, light, and elements**—not portraits.
- The tone is reverent, cosmic, and cinematic.
- **Wide-angle perspectives** and **grain-textured gradients** for depth.
- Use **halo light or luminous dust trails** to represent His divine presence.
- Merge **cultural textures (soil, rivers, cloth)** with **cosmic gradients**.

- Punjabi subtitles in calm, centered layout below main title.

## 🧰 GRAPHIC DETAILS

**Textures:**

- 10–20% film grain overlay
- Subtle vignette edges
- Faded matte colors (simulate print texture)
- Add halftone dot effect in background gradients for comic depth

**Lighting:**

- Always one light source behind Guru Nanak Dev Ji
- Use bloom or glow (subtle, gold or white)
- Never direct lighting on the face

**Composition:**

- Rule of thirds
- Horizon line always visible
- Depth of field (foreground blur optional)
